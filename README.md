# Activewear Manufacturer Research

A public-source research workflow for evaluating activewear manufacturers, factory evidence and bulk-production readiness.

This project is designed for apparel brands, wholesalers, importers and retail teams that are comparing suppliers for a real production plan.

## What this Skill helps with

The workflow helps a buyer or researcher:

- Compare activewear and sportswear manufacturers
- Check whether a factory's public claims are supported by evidence
- Separate verified facts, assumptions and items that still need confirmation
- Review MOQ conditions by style, colour, fabric and decoration
- Prepare questions before requesting a quotation
- Check the handover from sample approval to bulk production
- Review fabric, construction, packaging and repeat-order risks
- Organise a supplier comparison before contacting a factory

## Evidence rules

This project does not treat a polished website, a social-media post or a single factory photo as proof of production capability.

Each finding should be labelled as one of the following:

- **Verified fact** — supported by a public source or a supplied first-party record
- **Supplier claim** — stated by the manufacturer but not independently verified
- **Inference** — a reasonable interpretation that still needs confirmation
- **Open question** — information that should be requested before making a decision

Do not invent:

- MOQ figures
- Production capacity
- Sample or delivery times
- Certifications
- Customer names or order results
- Quality-control results
- Pricing or cost savings

## Buyer information to prepare

Before requesting a quotation, prepare:

- Product type
- Reference image, sample or tech pack
- Estimated quantity by style and colour
- Fabric requirements
- Branding, labels and packaging
- Target market
- Expected delivery date
- Questions that are still open

## Limitations

This repository is a public research aid. It does not replace factory due diligence, sample approval, quality inspection, contract review or independent verification.

Public information may be incomplete or outdated. Confirm current production conditions directly with the supplier before placing an order.
