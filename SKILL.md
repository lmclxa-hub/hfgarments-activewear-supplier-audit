# Activewear Manufacturer Research Skill

## Purpose

Use this skill to research activewear and sportswear manufacturers for brands with a real bulk-production or repeat-order plan.

The goal is to help buyers compare suppliers using public evidence, identify open questions and prepare the next verification step.

## Workflow

1. Define the buyer's product, quantity, target market and delivery target.
2. Collect public evidence from the supplier's website, product pages, factory pages and official social profiles.
3. Record the source URL and observation date for every material claim.
4. Separate verified facts, supplier claims, inferences and open questions.
5. Compare suppliers using the same product brief.
6. Check product fit, MOQ, sample approval, fabric, construction, packaging and reorder conditions.
7. Audit publicly available social profiles separately from factory evidence.
8. Prepare a buyer question list before requesting a quotation.
9. Do not recommend a supplier when key evidence is missing.

## Evidence labels

Use exactly one label for each finding:

- Verified fact — supported by a public source or supplied first-party record
- Supplier claim — stated by the supplier but not independently verified
- Inference — a reasonable interpretation that still needs confirmation
- Open question — information that should be requested before a decision

## Do not invent

Never invent or assume:

- MOQ figures
- Production capacity
- Sample or delivery times
- Certifications
- Customer names or order results
- Quality-control results
- Pricing or cost savings
- Delivery guarantees
- Social-media followers or activity numbers

## Social presence audit

When official social profiles are publicly available, audit them separately from factory evidence.

Check these platforms when applicable:

- LinkedIn
- Facebook
- Instagram
- YouTube
- TikTok
- X
- Pinterest

For each platform, record:

- Platform
- Official profile URL
- Account name and handle
- Followers or subscribers
- Total posts or videos, when publicly shown
- Earliest visible post or video date
- Most recent visible post or video date
- Videos published in the last 7 days
- Videos published in the last 30 days
- Whether the account appears active
- Source URL
- Observation date
- Access limitations

Use the following table:

| Platform | Official URL | Followers | Earliest visible post | Videos last 7 days | Videos last 30 days | Latest post | Evidence date | Limitation |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|

Social-audit rules:

- The earliest visible post is not necessarily the account creation date.
- Label it as “Earliest visible post found”.
- Count only publicly visible videos with a clear publication date.
- Do not count Stories, image posts or reposts as videos unless the platform identifies them as videos.
- Do not estimate hidden numbers.
- If data is unavailable, write “Not publicly shown”, “Not accessible” or “Could not verify”.
- Use one observation date and timezone for the whole audit.
- Social activity does not prove factory capacity, quality, delivery performance or customer results.

## Buyer information to prepare

Before requesting a quotation, prepare:

- Product type
- Reference image, sample or tech pack
- Estimated quantity by style and colour
- Fabric requirements
- Branding, labels and packaging
- Target market
- Expected delivery date
- Questions that are still open

## Example product categories

This workflow can be used for:

- Leggings and bike shorts
- Sports bras and activewear tops
- Training sets
- Compression garments
- Wide-leg pants and athleisure styles
- T-shirts and other private-label apparel

## Buyer output

Return:

- Supplier name and URL
- Product categories
- Social presence audit
- Evidence found
- Claims requiring confirmation
- Buyer risks
- Questions to ask
- Recommended next verification step

For each important claim, include the source URL and evidence label.

## Recommended verification request

For a specific production project, prepare:

- Product reference or tech pack
- Estimated quantity
- Fabric requirements
- Target market
- Expected delivery date
- Details that are still unconfirmed

Ask the supplier to confirm:

- MOQ by style, colour, fabric and decoration
- Sample fee, revision rules and sample timing
- Production lead-time starting point
- Material and performance documentation
- Quality standard and inspection points
- Outsourced processes
- Repeat-order and specification-control conditions

## Limitations

This repository is a public research aid. It does not replace factory due diligence, sample approval, quality inspection, contract review or independent verification.

Public information may be incomplete, inaccessible or outdated. Confirm current production conditions directly with the supplier before placing an order.
