# Activewear Manufacturer Research Skill

## Purpose

Use this skill to research activewear and sportswear manufacturers for brands with a real bulk-production or repeat-order plan.

## Workflow

1. Define the buyer's product, quantity, market and delivery target.
2. Collect public evidence from the manufacturer's website, product pages, factory pages and official profiles.
3. Record the source URL and date for every material claim.
4. Separate verified facts, supplier claims, inferences and open questions.
5. Compare suppliers using the same product brief.
6. Check MOQ, sample approval, fabric, construction, packaging and reorder conditions.
7. Prepare a buyer question list before requesting a quotation.
8. Do not make a supplier recommendation when key evidence is missing.

## Evidence labels

Use exactly one label for each finding:

- Verified fact
- Supplier claim
- Inference
- Open question

## Do not invent

Never invent MOQ, capacity, lead time, certification, customer result, pricing, quality result or delivery guarantee.
## Social presence audit

When official social profiles are publicly available, record them separately from factory evidence.

Check these platforms when applicable:

- LinkedIn
- Facebook
- Instagram
- YouTube
- TikTok
- X
- Pinterest

For each platform, record:

- Platform
- Official profile URL
- Account name and handle
- Followers or subscribers
- Following count, when publicly shown
- Total posts or videos, when publicly shown
- Earliest visible post or video date
- Most recent visible post or video date
- Videos published in the last 7 days
- Videos published in the last 30 days
- Whether the account appears active
- Source URL
- Observation date
- Access limitations

Do not estimate hidden or unavailable numbers.

If the platform does not show an exact count, write:

- Not publicly shown
- Not accessible
- Could not verify
- Approximate, only when the platform itself displays an approximation

The earliest visible post is not necessarily the account creation date. Label it as:

- Earliest visible post found

For video counts, count only publicly visible videos with a clear publication date. Do not treat reposts, Stories or image posts as videos unless the platform identifies them as videos.

Use this table:

| Platform | Official URL | Followers | Earliest visible post | Videos last 7 days | Videos last 30 days | Latest post | Evidence date | Limitation |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
